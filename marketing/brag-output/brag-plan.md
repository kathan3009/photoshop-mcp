# Brag Plan: Photoshop MCP

## What is this app?
A free Mac menu-bar app that connects Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor or Codex to the Photoshop already running on the Mac, then shows every edit the agent makes — with a checkpoint, a pause switch and undo.

## The angle
Apple product-film restraint applied to an agent tool. Dark canvas, one amber accent, big calm type, long settled holds, a voice that states things rather than sells them. The proof is the real thing: the app's own screens and a raw photo the agent actually graded through it. Nothing on screen is a mockup.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black. The graded Golden Gate frame fades up with a slow push. Two lines, one after the other: "Your agent can see." → "Now it can edit." The voice says the same two sentences. The second line's last word is amber.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The name reveal: the app icon lands, "Photoshop MCP" settles beneath it on a strong beat.
- The flow: a raw photo wipes into its graded version while five real tool calls tick in beside it — render, Select Subject, curves, mask, save. The product doing its thing.
- The kill switch: three guards stack in (checkpoint, your files stay yours, one undo per step) and the Pause button flips red on the beat.

## Outro / punchline
Icon + "Download for Mac" + kathandesai.com/photoshop-mcp. Voice: "Photoshop MCP. Free, for Mac." One bell. Hold. Fade.

## User flow worth showing
Ask the agent for a grade → it renders, selects the subject, builds masked adjustment layers, verifies, exports → graded JPEG next to the raw. Scene 3 is this flow (the before/after wipe is the real output of exactly that sequence).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: an Apple product film — quiet confidence, restraint, premium dark
- Interpretation: 5 scenes, settled holds, soft crossfades and slow pushes, no hard cuts except the kill-switch flip; copy is short and literal; the voice is calm and unhurried; SFX are two or three soft hits plus one bell.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24.6 seconds
