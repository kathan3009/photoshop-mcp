Photoshop

Let your AI agent work in Photoshop.

This app bundles the Photoshop MCP server. Connect it to a client once, keep this app running, and every edit the agent makes shows up here — with a pause switch and undo.

Connect

Copy Photoshop MCP to Applications, eject the installer, then open the installed app before connecting.

How it works

  1. Open Photoshop (any document).
  2. In your client, ask: “Run ps_status”. macOS asks once to allow controlling Photoshop — allow it.
  3. Then ask for real work: “Grade this photo warm and cinematic, non-destructively, and export a JPEG.”

Every edit is one undo step and rolls back on failure. A checkpoint is taken before the agent's first edit. The agent can't overwrite your files without overwrite: true.

Activity

Exports

Files the agent saved with ps_document save.

What the agent looked at

Every ps_render preview, newest first (last 300 kept).

Checkpoints

Layered copies of unsaved work, taken before the agent's first edit of a document. Newest 20 / 7 days are kept. Open one in Photoshop to recover.
